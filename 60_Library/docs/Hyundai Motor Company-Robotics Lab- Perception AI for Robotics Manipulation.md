**Who we are** We are responsible for developing AI models that enable robots to perceive and understand objects and environments based on camera inputs, empowering them with intelligent vision capabilities.  
  
**Responsibilities** In this role, you will have the opportunity to research and develop cutting-edge AI models for robotic perception, customize them to align with our specific environments and objectives, and translate your research into practical applications on real robotic systems.  
  
■ Perception  
  

- Conducting research on improving robot object and environment perception performance
- Visual prompting and in-context segmentation model research
- Spatial understanding research based on multi-modal models

■ 3D Vision  
  

- Developing 3D vision/AI-based object recognition algorithms
- Performance enhancement of target object pose estimation algorithms based on AI
- Synthetic data generation, augmentation, and filtering
- Algorithm integration and deployment on robotic frameworks

■ Grasping  
  

- Conducting AI research for grasping both structured and unstructured objects
- Grasping pose estimation model research
- Simulation-based grasping dataset construction and validation

**Minimum Qualifications**

- Master's degree in Electrical Engineering, AI, or Computer Science
- Minimum 5 years of experience in robotic manipulation AI research and development
- Hands-on experience applying developed AI models to real robotic platforms and validating their performance
- Experience in identifying limitations of existing algorithms, systematically designing experiments according to project objectives, and successfully implementing solutions
- Proficiency in Python and PyTorch, C/C++, and Git

**Preferred Qualifications**

- Experience with 2 or more publications at top-tier conferences (CVPR, ECCV, ICCV, etc.) or open-source contributions
- Experience with ROS2 framework (robot control and AI integration within ROS2 environments)
- Experience with large-scale model training (AI model training within GPU cluster environments)
- Experience achieving State-of-the-Art (SOTA) on global benchmarks
- Experience with NVIDIA platforms (NVIDIA Jetson controllers, TensorRT, etc.)
- Experience with Isaac Sim simulation (experience in data pipeline construction within Isaac Sim environments)
